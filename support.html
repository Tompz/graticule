Support

Answers to the questions people ask most, and how to reach me.

Adding a widget

  1. Touch and hold an empty part of your Home Screen until the apps jiggle.
  2. Tap Edit in the top corner, then Add Widget.
  3. Search for Graticule.
  4. Choose a widget, pick a size, and tap Add Widget.

A widget says it has no access, or shows dashes

It needs permission to read that part of your phone. Open Settings, then Apps, then Graticule, and turn on Calendars, Reminders or Health. For Health, open the Health app, tap your picture, then Apps, then Graticule, and allow Steps and Workouts. Steps shows dashes until there is a step count to show.

A widget looks out of date

iOS decides when widgets refresh, to save battery. Opening Graticule refreshes every widget straight away.

A widget is faint and says Graticule Plus

That widget is part of Graticule Plus. Tap it to see what Plus includes. Day, Outstanding and Steps are free.

Restoring Plus on a new phone

Open Graticule, then Settings, and tap Restore purchase. Plus follows your Apple Account, and Family Sharing members get it too.

Refunds

Apple handles refunds. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Widgets of your own

With Plus, open the Library and tap Copy the AI prompt. Paste it into any chatbot, followed by what you want, such as "a countdown to my holiday". Copy the chatbot's reply, return to the Library and tap Paste. Graticule checks it and shows you what it will read before anything is saved. If it is refused, the message says which line is wrong; paste that message back to the chatbot and it will fix it.

Contact

Report a problem or ask a question on GitHub. Please include your iPhone model, iOS version and the widget involved.
